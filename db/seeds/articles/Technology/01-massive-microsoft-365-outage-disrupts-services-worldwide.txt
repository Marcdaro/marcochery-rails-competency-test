---
title: "Massive Microsoft 365 Outage Disrupts Services Worldwide"
---

A widespread Microsoft 365 outage left businesses, government agencies, and millions of users struggling to access essential online services beginning Thursday, November 12, 2026.

The disruption affected several major Microsoft platforms, including Outlook, Microsoft Teams, Exchange Online, SharePoint, and Microsoft Copilot.

Users reported problems signing into accounts, sending emails, accessing documents, and communicating through workplace applications.

Microsoft identified infrastructure problems involving server health checks and increased processing loads, which caused portions of its cloud services to become unavailable.

The company implemented recovery measures, including restarting affected servers and adjusting infrastructure configurations.

Services improved throughout November 13 and 14, although some organizations continued experiencing delayed email deliveries during recovery.

The incident renewed concerns about how heavily businesses and public institutions depend on centralized cloud infrastructure.

For organizations worldwide, the outage served as another reminder of the importance of backup communication systems and business continuity planning.
