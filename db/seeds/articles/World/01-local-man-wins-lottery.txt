---
title: "Local Man Wins Lottery"
---

Local man (per confidentiality safety clause we will call him Man-Man) wins lottery after years of unsuccessful attempts.

Man-Man is credited for having now 'won in life'. Regardless of the exorbitant taxes that were taken as per gambling guidelines, he is pretty much set for the remainder of his life. It is said that Man-Man immediately quit his job and cut his family and friends off immediately after his sudden win.

Who knows what life has in store for Man-Man's next chapter?
