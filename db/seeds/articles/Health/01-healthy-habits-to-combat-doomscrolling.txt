---
title: "Healthy Habits to Combat Doomscrolling"
---

In an age of nonstop notifications and endless social media feeds, doomscrolling has become an increasingly common habit. The constant consumption of negative news and stressful content can leave people feeling anxious, overwhelmed, and mentally exhausted.

Fortunately, small lifestyle changes can help break the cycle.

Start by setting daily screen-time limits and turning off unnecessary notifications. Avoid checking social media immediately after waking up or right before bed, when your mind needs time to relax.

Replacing scrolling with productive activities such as reading, exercising, journaling, or spending time outdoors can make a significant difference.

Another helpful strategy is creating phone-free zones, particularly in bedrooms and during meals. Consider setting specific times to catch up on the news rather than repeatedly checking throughout the day.

Staying informed is important, but protecting your mental well-being matters just as much. Sometimes, the healthiest thing you can do is simply put your phone down.
