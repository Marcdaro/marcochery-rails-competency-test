---
title: "ChatGPT Is Changing Its Usage Limits in December — Will Users Have to Pay More?"
---

OpenAI has announced changes to how usage limits will work across ChatGPT and Codex beginning in December 2026.

Under the updated system, Plus, Pro, and Business subscribers will have a shared usage allowance across both services, with usage measured in tokens.

More advanced models and complex tasks will generally consume more of that allowance. However, regular Instant chats will not draw from it.

Does this mean every ChatGPT conversation will suddenly cost money?

No. Existing subscriptions will continue to include usage, and users will not automatically be charged for every message.

Those who reach their included limits can wait for their allowance to reset, upgrade their subscription, or purchase additional credits for eligible features.

OpenAI says most subscribers should not need to wait for resets more frequently than they currently do.

The changes reflect a broader shift toward flexible, usage-based access to increasingly powerful AI tools.
