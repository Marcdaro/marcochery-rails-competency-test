---
title: "Thousands of Flights Canceled After Nationwide FAA Ground Stop"
trending: 1
---

Air travelers across the United States faced widespread disruptions following a Federal Aviation Administration ground stop on Friday, November 13, 2026.

The nationwide order halted nearly all departing flights for almost five hours, triggering cancellations, delays, and significant scheduling problems for major airlines.

By Sunday, November 15, approximately 2,100 flights had been canceled and another 9,400 delayed, according to flight-tracking figures reported by Reuters.

Officials linked the disruption to communications equipment problems involving routers at FAA facilities in Arkansas and Texas.

Airlines spent the weekend repositioning aircraft, reorganizing flight crews, and assisting stranded passengers.

American Airlines announced that it expected to operate a largely normal schedule on Monday, November 16, as recovery efforts progressed.

The incident has renewed discussions about aging air traffic control infrastructure and the need for major technological upgrades.

Passengers with upcoming flights are encouraged to monitor airline notifications, confirm their flight status, and review available rebooking or refund options.
