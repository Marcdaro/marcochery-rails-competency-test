---
title: "Miami Heat Dominate Detroit Pistons in 137–117 Victory"
---

The Miami Heat continued their impressive winning streak Saturday, November 14, defeating the Detroit Pistons 137–117 in a commanding offensive performance.

Superstar Giannis Antetokounmpo led Miami with 34 points, 14 rebounds, and eight assists, showcasing his dominance on both ends of the court.

Klay Thompson added 26 points in his return from an ankle injury, while Bam Adebayo contributed another 20.

Detroit started strongly, taking a 39–37 lead after the first quarter. However, Miami responded by controlling the second and third periods, eventually building a comfortable lead.

Cade Cunningham led the Pistons with 33 points and eight assists, but Detroit struggled to contain Miami's transition offense.

The Heat finished with 14 blocked shots, just one shy of the franchise record, highlighting their defensive intensity.

The victory marked Miami's fourth consecutive win and improved the team's record to 7–6.

With Antetokounmpo and Thompson delivering strong performances, Miami is beginning to build momentum after a difficult start to the season.
