---
title: "UEFA Champions League Standings: PSG and Real Madrid Lead the Race"
---

The 2026–27 UEFA Champions League is heating up as Europe's biggest clubs battle for positions in the league phase.

Following Matchday 4, Paris Saint-Germain and Real Madrid sit at the top with 12 points each, maintaining perfect records through four matches.

Arsenal follows in third place with 10 points, while several clubs remain closely grouped behind the leaders.

Current top eight:

Paris Saint-Germain — 12 points
Real Madrid — 12 points
Arsenal — 10 points
Como — 9 points
Barcelona — 9 points
Aston Villa — 9 points
Manchester City — 9 points
Liverpool — 9 points

Paris Saint-Germain's impressive attacking performances have given the French champions an early advantage on goal difference.

With four league-phase matches remaining for each team, the race for automatic qualification is far from settled.

The top eight teams advance directly to the Round of 16, while clubs ranked ninth through 24th enter knockout-phase playoffs.

The league phase concludes January 27, 2027.
