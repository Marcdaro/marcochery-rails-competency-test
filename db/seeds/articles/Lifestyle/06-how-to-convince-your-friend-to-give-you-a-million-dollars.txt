title: "How to convince your friend to give you a million dollars"
---

Friendship is all about the love and appreciation you have for the other and the varying lengths you might go to be there for the other person. Sometimes in every friendship, you must test the limits of what they would do for you out of sheer appreciation and what better way than to ask for a million dollars. Assuming you’re friends with Jeff Bezos, then the process should be relatively straightforward. If you haven’t established a relationship with Jeff Bezos, then I suggest you send him a friend request on Facebook then refer back to this guide. Good luck you amazing friend!
