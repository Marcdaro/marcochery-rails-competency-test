---
title: "UEFA Nations League: France Tops Group as England Stuns Spain"
---

The UEFA Nations League delivered a dramatic Sunday of international football on November 15, with major results shaping the knockout-stage picture.

France secured first place in League A Group 1 following a 2–1 victory over Türkiye in Bordeaux.

An own goal from Emirhan Topçu and a late strike from Eli Junior Kroupi gave France the advantage, while Abdülkerim Bardakcı scored Türkiye's only goal.

Belgium also impressed, defeating Italy 4–1 to secure second place in the group.

Meanwhile, England produced one of the day's biggest results, defeating Spain 3–2 in Madrid and ending the world champions' lengthy unbeaten run.

Group A1 final standings:

France — 13 points
Belgium — 12 points
Italy — 10 points
Türkiye — 0 points

In Group A3, England and Spain finished level on 15 points, with England taking first place on goal difference.

France, Belgium, England, and Spain have all secured quarter-final qualification as the remaining Nations League groups prepare to conclude their fixtures.
