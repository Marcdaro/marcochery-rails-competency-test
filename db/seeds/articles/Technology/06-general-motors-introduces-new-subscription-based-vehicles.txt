title: "General Motors Introduces New Subscription-Based Vehicles — Drivers Must Watch TWO Unskippable Ads Before Starting Their Cars"
---

General Motors has reportedly revolutionized the automotive industry by introducing a groundbreaking new feature nobody asked for: microtransactions, mandatory advertisements, and subscription-based vehicle access.

Under GM's new "DrivePass+" program, drivers must watch TWO consecutive 30-second unskippable advertisements before their dashboard becomes operational.

Want to see your speedometer? That'll be $9.99/month.

Need access to your backup camera? Purchase the Parking Expansion Pack for $14.99.

The base vehicle includes a complimentary 7-day trial of the steering wheel.

However, the company's most controversial decision involves its new KeyPass Premium subscription. For just $24.99/month, customers can unlock the revolutionary ability to START THEIR FREAKING CAR.

"We believe vehicle ownership should be an ongoing financial relationship," explained a fictional GM spokesperson. "Why sell someone a car once when we can sell it to them every month?"

Drivers without an active subscription will reportedly receive a notification stating:

"Your vehicle has been temporarily disabled. Please renew your subscription or watch this 90-second ad to unlock one ignition attempt."

GM has also announced that turn signals, windshield wipers, and air conditioning will be available through separate downloadable content packs.

The company insists these changes are designed to "enhance the customer experience."

The check engine light, however, remains completely free.
