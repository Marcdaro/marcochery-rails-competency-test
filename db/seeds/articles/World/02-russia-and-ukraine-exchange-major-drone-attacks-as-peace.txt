---
title: "Russia and Ukraine Exchange Major Drone Attacks as Peace Talks Stall"
---

Fighting between Russia and Ukraine continues to intensify as both countries exchange large-scale aerial attacks despite ongoing diplomatic efforts to end the war.

On November 15, Ukrainian officials reported Russian strikes across at least nine regions, targeting civilian infrastructure, railway facilities, and energy systems.

According to Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy, at least 16 people were injured in the overnight attacks.

Ukraine also launched major drone operations against Russian targets, including an earlier reported wave involving nearly 1,200 drones.

Meanwhile, United States-led diplomatic discussions have failed to produce a significant breakthrough.

Negotiations have expanded to include broader European security arrangements, raising concerns among several European governments about the future of regional security.

As winter approaches, damage to power infrastructure remains a serious concern for Ukrainian communities already struggling with prolonged disruptions.

The continued fighting highlights the challenges of reaching a lasting peace agreement while military operations remain active on both sides.
