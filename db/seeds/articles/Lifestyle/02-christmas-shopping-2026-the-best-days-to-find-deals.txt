---
title: "Christmas Shopping 2026: The Best Days to Find Deals"
---

The holiday shopping season is approaching, and shoppers are preparing for some of the biggest sales of the year.

Black Friday officially falls on Friday, November 27, followed by Cyber Monday on Monday, November 30.

However, bargain hunters won't necessarily need to wait until Thanksgiving weekend to start saving.

Major retailers are rolling out early holiday promotions throughout November, offering discounts on electronics, clothing, home appliances, and gifts.

Here's a quick look at the important dates:

November 19: Amazon's Black Friday Week begins.
November 27: Black Friday.
November 28: Small Business Saturday.
November 30: Cyber Monday.

Black Friday can be especially useful for electronics and high-demand gifts, while Cyber Monday is worth watching for online promotions.

Before checking out, compare prices across retailers, review return policies, and be skeptical of inflated discount claims.

Planning ahead could help you finish your Christmas shopping early without overspending.
