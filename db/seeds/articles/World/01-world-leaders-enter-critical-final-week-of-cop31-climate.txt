---
title: "World Leaders Enter Critical Final Week of COP31 Climate Talks"
---

Global climate negotiations are entering a decisive stage as COP31 moves into its final week in Antalya, Türkiye.

The United Nations climate conference, running from November 9 through November 20, has brought together government representatives, environmental organizations, and international leaders to address the growing challenges of climate change.

Major discussions center on reducing greenhouse gas emissions, transitioning away from fossil fuels, and increasing financial support for countries facing severe climate impacts.

However, significant disagreements remain.

Developing nations are calling for stronger financial commitments from wealthier countries, while disagreements over fossil fuel policies continue to complicate negotiations.

A proposed initiative to expand electricity's share of global energy consumption by 2035 has also gained attention.

With negotiations scheduled to conclude Friday, participating nations face mounting pressure to produce meaningful agreements.

The conference's outcome could influence international climate policy and clean-energy investment for years to come.
