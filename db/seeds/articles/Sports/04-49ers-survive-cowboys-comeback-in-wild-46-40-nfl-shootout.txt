---
title: "49ers Survive Cowboys Comeback in Wild 46–40 NFL Shootout"
---

The San Francisco 49ers outlasted the Dallas Cowboys in a thrilling 46–40 victory Sunday, November 15, delivering one of the most explosive offensive performances of the NFL weekend.

Christian McCaffrey led San Francisco's attack with three touchdowns and 223 yards from scrimmage, including touchdown receptions of 74 and 66 yards.

Quarterback Brock Purdy threw for a career-high 389 yards and three touchdowns, while Deebo Samuel added a spectacular 70-yard rushing touchdown.

Dallas refused to go down quietly.

Cowboys quarterback Dak Prescott threw for 343 yards and five touchdowns, with receiver George Pickens catching three scores for 145 yards.

Despite a late Dallas comeback, San Francisco held on to secure the victory.

The 49ers generated an astonishing 584 total offensive yards, exposing significant weaknesses in the Cowboys' defense.

With the win, San Francisco improved to 8–1, further strengthening its position among the NFC's leading contenders.
